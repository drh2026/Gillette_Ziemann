Privacy Policy

The White House Cook Book 1887 App  ·  Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the White House Cook Book 1887 app (“the App”), developed and maintained by Dr. Samer Hassan (“I,” “me,” or “my”), handles information in connection with your use of the App.

1. No Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. I do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, usage statistics, or any other data.

2. No Account Required

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. All content is available immediately upon installation with no registration of any kind.

3. No Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-sharing services. No data is transmitted to any external server.

4. No Internet Required

All 1,131 recipes and all App content are bundled locally within the App. No internet connection is required to use the App. The App does not make any network requests.

Note: External archive.org page links within individual recipe entries open in your device browser and are subject to the Internet Archive’s own privacy policy if accessed.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App contains no content inappropriate for any age and does not collect information from any user, including children under the age of 13. The App is rated 4+ on the Apple App Store.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App’s data practices change. Any updates will be reflected in the App Store listing and at this URL. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will indicate when revisions were made.
